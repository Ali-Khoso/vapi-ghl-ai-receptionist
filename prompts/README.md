Use `john-williamson.md` for new configurations. It consistently identifies John Williamson at the example business BrightFix Home Services, confirms all required fields, and distinguishes receiving contact details from booking or dispatch.

`john-williamson-original.md` is the author's preserved source. Its scheduling, manual follow-up, and exactly-once wording describe intentions that the supplied integration does not enforce. It remains unchanged for provenance; do not combine both prompts in an assistant.

When adapting the business identity, update the active prompt and Vapi first message together. The existing demo assets are historical evidence and may show another business greeting; they are not a preview of the revised prompt or proof of a successful CRM write.
